Hiking shoes or boots: which do you need?

Updated 18 November 2026

The right footwear depends on the terrain and the weight on your back, not on how long the trip is.

In short

  • Low hiking shoes suit trails and a light pack.
  • Boots give support in rough terrain and with a heavy pack.
  • Try shoes on in the afternoon, with the socks you will use.

Low hiking shoes

Low hiking shoes are light and soft and leave your legs less tired on good trails. They give little ankle support and let water in more easily from above.

Boots

A high cuff and a stiffer sole give support on scree, bog and steep ground. You notice it most when the pack is heavy.

With or without a membrane

A waterproof membrane keeps feet dry in wet grass and bog. Shoes without one are airier and dry faster once they have got wet.

Fit

Feet swell a little through the day. Check that your toes do not hit the front going downhill, and that the heel stays put.
