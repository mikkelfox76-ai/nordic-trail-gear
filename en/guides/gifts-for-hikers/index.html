Gifts for hikers: ideas at every price

Updated 18 November 2026

The best outdoor gift is the one that gets used on every trip. Here are ideas from under NOK 300 to over NOK 1,500, with what to know before you buy.

In short

  • Wool, light and a hot drink get used on almost every winter trip.
  • Choose neutral sizes and colours, or check the return policy.
  • Find out quietly what is already in their pack, so you do not give a duplicate.

Under NOK 300

Good wool socks are a safe gift. Merino wool keeps insulating when damp, and a terry knit in the foot traps air that holds the warmth. Pick a medium thickness so they work in both hiking boots and ski boots.

NOK 300 to 700

This range covers headlamps, flasks and wool mittens. A headlamp that takes ordinary AAA batteries is easy to live with, and lithium cells handle cold better than alkaline. Mittens are warmer than gloves because the fingers warm each other. A flask keeps heat best when you warm it with hot water before filling it.

NOK 700 to 1,500

A light stove set or a large power bank opens the way to longer trips. A gas stove with a pot that nests inside takes little room in the pack. A 20,000 mAh power bank charges a phone several times, but delivers less in the cold and should ride in an inside pocket.

Over NOK 1,500

For something that lasts many years, a thick wool jacket is a good choice. Wool terry at 400 grams per square metre is made for cold days and works as a mid layer under a shell. Check size and fit, and whether the shop extends returns over Christmas.
