Sleeping bags: how to read the temperature ratings

Updated 18 November 2026

Sleeping bags carry three temperature figures. You should plan around only one of them.

In short

  • Plan around the comfort temperature.
  • The extreme figure is about survival, not sleep.
  • The pad matters as much as the bag.

Comfort, limit and extreme

Comfort is the temperature at which a cold sleeper sleeps well. Limit is where a warm sleeper gets by curled up. Extreme only states what a person survives.

Down or synthetic

Down is lightest and packs smallest for its warmth but insulates poorly when wet. Synthetic fill copes better with damp and costs less, but is heavier and bulkier.

Fit

A bag that is too big leaves a lot of air for your body to heat. One that is too tight squashes the fill, which then insulates less.

The rest of the system

The fill underneath you is compressed and insulates little. Without a good pad, even a warm bag feels cold.
