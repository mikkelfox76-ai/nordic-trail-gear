About Nordic Trail Gear

Nordic Trail Gear publishes guides to outdoor gear for Nordic conditions: hiking equipment, clothing and electronics. We compare specs, prices and reviews, and explain what matters when you choose. We say so when we have not handled a product ourselves.

This site is funded by affiliate links. When you click one and buy from the store, we may earn a commission. The price you pay is the same. Affiliate links are labelled next to each button, and the order in our guides follows our assessment, not the commission.

Privacy

We do not collect personal data on this site and use no tracking tools of our own. When you click an affiliate link, the store and the affiliate network may set cookies to record that the purchase came from here. That is covered by their own privacy policies.
