How to choose a tent for Nordic conditions

Updated 18 November 2026

Wind, rain and bright nights make their own demands on a tent. Settle these points before you choose.

In short

  • Choose the season rating by the worst trip you plan.
  • Tunnel tents give the most space for the weight, dome tents stand firm on any ground.
  • A tent pitched fly-first keeps the inner dry in rain.

Season

Three-season tents are light and airy and cover spring, summer and autumn below the treeline. Four-season tents handle snow and strong wind but weigh more.

Tunnel or dome

Tunnel tents give a lot of space for the weight but must be pitched end-on to the wind and need good pegs. Dome tents are freestanding and easier on rock and hard ground.

Size and weight

A two-person tent is tight for two with gear. Many people size up by one. A vestibule gives room for packs and wet boots.

Pitching in rain

Tents where fly and inner go up together, or fly first, keep the sleeping area dry when you make camp in bad weather.
