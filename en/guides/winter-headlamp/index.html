Headlamps for winter trips: what to look for

Updated 18 November 2026

Dark months and cold put different demands on a headlamp than a summer evening. Here is what actually matters when you choose.

In short

  • Battery matters more than peak brightness in the cold.
  • Pick a lamp you can operate with mittens on.
  • Check water resistance (IP rating) before you look at price.

Battery in the cold

Batteries deliver less power when it is cold. A lamp with a battery pack you can keep inside your jacket lasts longer on winter trips.

Brightness and beam

Lumens tell you how much light the lamp gives in total. Skiing and navigation need long reach, while camp chores need a wide, even beam.

Controls

Large buttons or dials work with mittens. A lock stops the lamp switching on inside your pack.

Weight and fit

A heavy lamp should have a top strap. Check that it sits firmly over a hat or helmet.
